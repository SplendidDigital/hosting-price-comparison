# Hosting Price Comparison

**A WordPress plugin by Digital Splendid**

Hosting Price Comparison helps website owners organize hosting providers and plans, compare hosting prices and features, track price history, and check listed prices against information available from official plan pages.

The plugin provides an administrator interface for maintaining hosting plan details and a public comparison table that helps visitors evaluate hosting options.

## Features

- **Hosting provider management:** Organize hosting providers and their plans.
- **Hosting plan management:** Add and edit plan names, plan types, pricing, billing periods, storage, website limits, and other plan details.
- **Introductory pricing:** Record introductory offers, upfront totals, and introductory term durations.
- **Flexible billing periods:** Support monthly, yearly, and custom billing periods with configurable units and durations.
- **Renewal pricing:** Record renewal prices and billing periods to help visitors understand costs after introductory offers expire.
- **Effective price calculations:** Display monthly and yearly equivalents when the required pricing information is available.
- **Hosting feature comparisons:** Present plan features, storage, RAM, CPU, traffic limits, and other available specifications.
- **Affiliate and signup links:** Link provider or plan names to the saved plan/signup URL, including affiliate URLs where applicable.
- **Official source links:** Provide links to official plan pages for visitors and price verification.
- **Price verification:** Check saved plan pricing against amounts detected on accessible source pages.
- **Verification status:** Display verification results and the last-check information.
- **Price history:** Keep a record of pricing changes when plans are saved with updated prices.
- **Public comparison table:** Display hosting plan information through a WordPress shortcode.
- **Plan visibility controls:** Manage whether hosting plans are publicly displayed.

## Pricing transparency

Hosting plans can have introductory offers that differ significantly from their regular or renewal prices.

Hosting Price Comparison separates these amounts so visitors can better understand:

- The introductory amount paid upfront.
- The duration covered by the introductory offer.
- The equivalent monthly and yearly costs.
- The regular comparison price.
- The renewal price and billing period.
- The features and resources included in each plan.

Calculated equivalents depend on the pricing information entered by the administrator. Missing or incomplete billing information may prevent a reliable calculation.

## Price verification

The plugin includes a price-verification workflow that compares saved pricing information with amounts detected on an accessible source page.

Verification results help administrators review whether a listed price matches information found on the source page.

**Important:** Automated verification is not a guarantee that a price is correct or current. Pages may contain multiple prices, promotional offers, regional pricing, taxes, or prices for different billing terms. Results should be reviewed when necessary.

A successful match does not necessarily mean that every plan detail has been verified.

## Installation

1. Download the plugin ZIP from the repository's Releases section.
2. Sign in to your WordPress administration dashboard.
3. Navigate to **Plugins → Add Plugin → Upload Plugin**.
4. Upload the ZIP file.
5. Install and activate the plugin.
6. Open the Hosting Prices administration area.
7. Add hosting providers and plans, including their pricing, billing periods, features, and relevant URLs.
8. Add the comparison shortcode to a WordPress page, following the instructions included with the installed release.

Back up your website before installing or updating the plugin.
